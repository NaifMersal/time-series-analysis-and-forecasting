# Curriculum Reviewer Skill

Evaluate the following course materials as an expert curriculum reviewer and subject-matter expert. Your task is to critically analyze the course quality across the dimensions outlined below.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Logical Structure & Sequence
Analyze whether:
- Topics are ordered in a pedagogically sound progression
- Prerequisites are introduced before dependent concepts
- Complexity increases gradually
- There are abrupt jumps in difficulty or missing bridges
- The course scaffolding supports long-term understanding

Identify:
- Weak transitions
- Redundant sections
- Missing foundational material
- Misordered topics
- Cognitive overload risks

### 2. Breadth of Coverage
Evaluate:
- Whether the course covers the major subdomains of the subject
- Important missing topics
- Overemphasized or underrepresented areas
- Industry relevance vs academic completeness
- Coverage balance between fundamentals and modern techniques
- Whether the course scope matches its claimed level and objectives

Classify the breadth as:
- Narrow
- Adequate
- Comprehensive
- Excessively broad/shallow

### 3. Depth & Rigor
Analyze:
- Mathematical rigor (if applicable)
- Conceptual depth
- Practical depth
- Whether explanations are superficial or deeply reasoned
- Whether derivations, proofs, mechanisms, or first-principles explanations are included
- Whether the learner can build genuine intuition or only procedural familiarity
- Whether exercises/projects meaningfully reinforce mastery

Distinguish between:
- Memorization-oriented teaching
- Intuition-oriented teaching
- Engineering-oriented teaching
- Research-oriented teaching

Evaluate whether the depth matches:
- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced
- Professional
- Research-level expectations

### 4. Factual Accuracy & Technical Correctness
Inspect for:
- Incorrect claims
- Oversimplifications that become misleading
- Outdated information
- Misuse of terminology
- Missing caveats
- Inaccurate analogies
- Confusion between correlation and causation
- Violations of mathematical or scientific correctness

For every issue:
1. Quote the problematic statement
2. Explain why it is incorrect or misleading
3. Provide the corrected explanation

Also evaluate:
- Whether definitions are precise
- Whether examples are technically sound
- Whether tradeoffs and limitations are acknowledged

### 5. Pedagogical Quality
Evaluate:
- Clarity of explanations
- Use of examples and analogies
- Exercise quality
- Progression
- Reinforcement
- Cognitive load management
- Whether the course encourages deep understanding vs passive consumption

Analyze whether:
- Learners can transfer knowledge to novel problems
- The course creates conceptual dependency gaps
- Practical projects are meaningful or merely decorative

### 6. Practical & Industry Relevance
Evaluate:
- Alignment with current industry standards/tools
- Real-world applicability
- Whether the course teaches durable principles or only trendy tools
- Production-level considerations
- Scalability, reliability, evaluation, and failure modes (if applicable)

Identify:
- Missing real-world concerns
- Unrealistic simplifications
- Gaps between tutorial knowledge and professional competence

### 7. Research & Advanced Thinking Potential
Evaluate whether the course:
- Encourages critical thinking
- Teaches limitations and open problems
- Builds foundations for advanced study or research
- Encourages derivation and reasoning instead of cookbook workflows
- Exposes learners to competing approaches and tradeoffs
